Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 June 2026

Overview

The Dundee Recovery Map is a static web application. It does not collect, store, or process any personal data directly, with the exception of information voluntarily submitted via the contact form. This policy explains what limited data may be handled and by whom.

Data

No personal data is collected directly through use of the map. There are no user accounts, logins, or analytics tracking on this site. The only personal data collected is that which is voluntarily provided via the contact form, described below.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies. No tracking cookies, session cookies, or preference cookies are used.

Hosting

This site is served via a custom domain and hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic server access data (such as IP addresses) as part of their standard hosting infrastructure. Please refer to GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Third-party services

The map uses the following services:

  • Leaflet.js - an open-source mapping library
  • OpenStreetMap - open map tiles. OpenStreetMap's own privacy policy applies to tile requests.
  • Formspree - provides an endpoint to collect submissions from the contact form.

Service directory data

The service listings on this map contain only publicly available information about organisations and services, including names, addresses, phone numbers, and opening hours. This information is sourced from publicly available directories.

If you represent a service listed on this map and would like to request a correction or removal, please contact me.

Contact form

If you use the contact form on this site, the following data is collected and processed:

  • Name - to address your reply
  • Email address - to respond to your enquiry
  • Message content - to understand and respond to your request

This data is submitted securely via Fromspree, a third-party form processing service. Fromspree acts as a data processor on our behalf. Submissions are retained by Fromspree for up to 30 days on their free tier. Please refer to Fromspree's Privacy Policy for details of how they handle data.

Your contact data will not be shared with any third party beyond Fromspree's processing role, and will only be used to respond to your enquiry.

You have the right to request deletion of any submission. To do so, please contact me with the details of your submission.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the nature of the site changes, for example, if analytics are introduced in a future version. The date at the top of this page reflects when it was last revised.
